These terms explain the use of our website, printing services, Store Supplies ordering, and related account features. Please review them before accepting or placing an order. Keep your order reference, specifications, payment records, and relevant shop messages for future inquiries.

1. Accounts and account security

Provide accurate contact and account information so the shop can identify your order and communicate with you. Keep your password, verification codes, and account access confidential. Use an account you own or are authorized to manage, sign out on shared devices, and report suspected unauthorized access through shop support.

Google sign-in and email/password sign-in use the account identity recognized by the system. Contact the administrator if your orders, RFID association, or points appear under an incorrect account so the identity records can be reviewed.

2. Order information and approval

Review the selected service or product, specifications, quantity, uploaded files, pickup arrangement, payment method, and total before submitting. An order submission enters the shop's review queue. The order status and shop confirmation communicate whether it has been approved, is being processed, or is ready for pickup.

If a price, stock level, file requirement, or specification changes before the order is accepted, review the updated information with the shop before proceeding. Use the order reference when requesting corrections or clarifications.

3. Print files, proofing, and specifications

Customers are responsible for checking the dimensions, spelling, and quality of uploaded files or layouts before submitting a print order.

Check page order, page count, copies, orientation, paper size, color mode, dimensions, image resolution, and any service-specific requirements. Explain special instructions in the order note. Ask the shop to clarify a requirement before approval when the preview or specifications are unclear.

Screen settings, paper, ink, and production methods can affect color and appearance. Discuss an exact color, material, finish, or measurement requirement with the shop before approval. The shop remains responsible for fulfilling the agreed specifications and addressing verified production defects under applicable law.

4. Content ownership and permitted use

Submit files you own or have permission to reproduce. Permission to use submitted content is limited to the activities reasonably needed to review, process, print, fulfill, and support the order. Ownership of the customer's original content remains with its owner.

Use the service lawfully and respect copyright, trademarks, personal information, and other people's rights. The shop may request clarification or decline content where reproduction would violate applicable law or another person's rights. Contact the shop if a document contains sensitive information requiring special handling.

5. Prices, quantities, and Store Supplies

Review the displayed unit price, selected quantity, subtotal, applicable points discount, and amount payable before confirming. Store Supplies availability depends on the current inventory and order review. Raise discrepancies with the shop before approval or payment.

Product images and descriptions help identify the item. Confirm any essential brand, model, size, material, or other specification with the shop. Any proposed substitution or additional charge should be discussed with the customer before proceeding.

6. Payments and payment verification

New customers use GCash for their first paid order. Cash on Pickup unlocks after an order linked to the account has been payment-verified and completed. Pending, approved, processing, ready-for-pickup, cancelled, rejected, and fully free reward orders leave this requirement outstanding.

Use the payment options and merchant details displayed during checkout. For GCash, check the recipient details and amount before sending, then provide the requested reference and any supporting receipt. Keep your transaction record and contact the shop promptly about an incorrect payment.

The shop manually reviews GCash payments and confirms payment received where required. A submitted reference, receipt, order status, or notification alone is insufficient proof that funds were received. Cash on Pickup is settled with the shop according to the confirmed order amount.

7. Customer cancellation before approval

Orders may only be cancelled before admin approval. Approved orders can no longer be cancelled through the customer cancellation feature.

Use Cancel Order while an eligible order remains Order Submitted or For Approval. The system rechecks its current state when processing the request. If approval is completed first, contact the shop to discuss the situation. The saved Cancelled status confirms a successful cancellation and keeps the record available in order history.

A cancellation request and a refund decision are separate processes. Contact the shop with the order and payment reference about money already sent. Statutory remedies for defects, incorrect fulfillment, or other legally recognized grounds remain available.

8. Refund review and order problems

A cancelled GCash order may be marked Needs Review so the shop can assess the payment and any refund request. The review result may be Refunded or No Refund Needed. Ask the shop for clarification and supporting payment details if the result is unclear.

Please report missing items, damage, incorrect specifications, payment discrepancies, or production problems through shop support. Include the order reference, a description, and relevant photographs or receipts. The appropriate repair, replacement, refund, or other remedy depends on the circumstances and applicable consumer law.

Customer cancellation limits, customized work, and pickup policies are applied together with the consumer rights and remedies required by law.

9. Processing time and notifications

Printing usually takes 1–2 business days, depending on the quantity and service. Bulk orders, including tarpaulins and shirt printing, may require additional time.

Discuss a required deadline with the shop before approval. Timing can depend on file readiness, agreed specifications, materials, order volume, payment review, equipment availability, and other production conditions. Check the latest order status or contact the shop about changes to an agreed schedule.

Messages and notifications depend on connectivity, device permissions, and service availability. Check My Orders for the current record and confirm with the shop when a deadline or pickup arrangement is important.

10. Pickup and unclaimed printed items

Collect your order after the shop confirms it is ready. Bring the order reference and relevant payment details, and tell the shop if another person will collect it for you. Check the item and agreed specifications at pickup and raise any concerns promptly.

The shop's existing pickup policy allows disposal of printed items left unclaimed for 30 calendar days after completion. Contact the shop before that period ends if you need to discuss collection. Handling of unclaimed items remains subject to applicable privacy requirements, consumer rights, and any unresolved order complaint.

11. Loyalty points, rewards, and RFID

Eligible registered customers earn one point per paid and completed order. A POS sale must be linked to the registered account to qualify. Guest purchases and fully free reward claims are outside the earning conditions. RFID identifies an account and records card activity. Eligible completed purchases determine earned points.

Whole points may be used for a discount of up to 50% of the order subtotal, subject to the available balance and current conversion shown at checkout. A catalog reward is a separate claim with its displayed points cost, quantity, specifications, and availability. One reward type applies per transaction.

Pending orders can reserve points. Approval finalizes the deduction. Eligible cancellation releases the reservation, and rejection returns applicable reserved or used points through the system's recorded process. Contact the shop about a balance discrepancy and provide the relevant order reference.

Review the displayed conversion and reward requirements before confirming each transaction. Report a lost RFID card or incorrect account association to the administrator for review.

The system processes account and contact details, order specifications, uploaded documents, payment references or receipts, support messages, RFID activity, loyalty records, and notification device tokens as needed for authentication, fulfillment, support, security, and loyalty administration.

Firebase and Supabase provide infrastructure used by these features. Authorized shop personnel handle records according to their operational responsibilities. Customer documents and receipts use controlled access. Public catalog images are intended for public display.

Retention should remain proportionate to fulfillment, support, security, and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Contact the shop through its support chat or counter to request information, access, correction, or other applicable privacy rights. Privacy concerns may also be raised with the National Privacy Commission.
